About FacadePro Academy

Bridging field expertise with practical online education for the exterior cleaning industry.

Who We Are

FacadePro Academy was founded by experienced facade and exterior cleaning professionals who saw a need for clear, practical training that goes beyond generic cleaning courses.

We focus on real-world methods for brick, stone, render, cladding, glass, and mixed surfaces — with strong emphasis on safety, efficiency, and client-ready results.

Our online classes are designed to be personalized. When you enroll, you provide your background and goals so we can tailor content, examples, and support to you or your team.

🎯

Our Mission

To raise standards in facade cleaning through accessible, high-quality online training that respects both craftsmanship and safety.

👷

Our Approach

Combine proven field techniques with structured modules, live Q&A options, and customization based on your experience and equipment.

📈

Who We Serve

Independent cleaners, small crews, facility maintenance teams, and contractors looking to expand or formalize facade services.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Industry practitioners as instructors — content drawn from actual project experience, not pure theory.
  • Personalized online delivery — after you submit your details, we shape the class around your level and objectives.
  • Clear safety focus — chemical handling, equipment use, and high-rise considerations included where relevant.
  • Transparent pricing and process — no hidden fees; you choose a class, share information, and we contact you to finalize the personalized program.
